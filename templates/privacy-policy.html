Privacy Policy

At SmartFiQ AI Solutions, safeguarding customer data, API telemetry, and conversational intelligence is foundational to our enterprise architecture.

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

database 1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by enterprise clients, website visitors, and authorized API integrators:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, work email address, phone number, company name, and billing details provided during consultation booking or subscription.
  • API & Integration Telemetry: Log data, webhook requests, timestamp meta, and conversation payloads routed through WhatsApp Business API or AI Voice Agent triggers.
  • Device & Analytics Meta: Anonymized IP addresses, browser fingerprint, session duration, and page view navigation to optimize platform speed and security.

shield 2. AI Processing Safeguards & Model Training

SmartFiQ connects to enterprise-tier Large Language Model (LLM) providers under strict Zero Data Retention (ZDR) and Non-Training Service Level Agreements:

Zero Training Policy:

Your proprietary business records, client chat logs, and vector database embeddings are NEVER submitted to public LLM datasets for model retraining. Your data remains strictly isolated to your tenant sandbox.

gavel 3. GDPR & International Privacy Compliance

We comply with global privacy frameworks including GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and Indian DPDP Act:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete export of your stored leads, chat logs, or analytics history at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget Me): Upon request, all data associated with your organization will be permanently deleted from our primary servers and backups within 30 days.
  • Data Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 AES-256 encryption, and data at rest is encrypted using FIPS 140-2 validated cryptographic keys.

schedule 4. Data Retention & Third-Party Vendors

We retain client logs only for the minimum operational timeframe required to maintain workflow state and analytics accuracy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your business data to third-party ad networks or brokers.
